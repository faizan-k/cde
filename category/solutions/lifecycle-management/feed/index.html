Buildings undergo gradual deterioration due to environmental exposure, usage, and aging. Without regular evaluation, minor issues can develop into major structural and maintenance problems.

A building condition audit provides a comprehensive understanding of the current state of a structure and supports informed decisions for maintenance and asset management.

What is Building Condition Audit

A building condition audit is a systematic inspection and evaluation of a structure to identify defects, assess risks, and determine maintenance requirements.

It covers structural elements as well as related components that affect performance and safety.

Our Approach

1. Comprehensive Inspection

A detailed inspection of structural elements is carried out to identify defects such as cracks, spalling, leakage, and signs of deterioration.

2. Condition Evaluation

Each component is assessed to determine its condition, severity of damage, and potential risks.

3. Risk Assessment

Defects are evaluated based on their impact on safety, performance, and durability.

4. Documentation

All findings are recorded in a structured format with clear descriptions and supporting data.

5. Maintenance Planning Input

Recommendations are developed to guide maintenance and repair strategies.

Scope of Services

  • Inspection of structural and related elements
  • Identification and classification of defects
  • Risk evaluation and prioritization
  • Maintenance and repair recommendations
  • Preparation of audit reports

Key Outcomes

  • Clear understanding of building condition

  • Improved maintenance planning

Understanding the true condition of a structure is the foundation of any successful engineering intervention. Over time, structures are exposed to environmental effects, material degradation, and usage-related stresses that may not always be visible on the surface.

A professional condition assessment provides clarity—helping stakeholders make informed decisions regarding repair, strengthening, or continued service.

What is Condition Assessment?

Condition assessment is a systematic evaluation of a structure to determine its current state, identify defects, and understand the underlying causes of deterioration.

At CDE, we go beyond visual inspection. Our approach combines engineering expertise, testing methodologies, and analytical tools to provide a complete understanding of structural performance.

Our Approach

1. Visual Inspection

We conduct detailed on-site inspections to identify visible defects such as cracks, spalling, corrosion signs, and surface deterioration.

2. Review of Documents

Existing drawings, specifications, and construction records are analyzed to understand design intent and historical performance.

3. Testing & Investigation

We employ non-destructive and, where necessary, destructive testing methods to evaluate material properties and structural integrity.

4. Defect Mapping

All observations are systematically recorded and mapped to identify patterns and severity.

5. Root Cause Analysis

Identifying the cause of deterioration is critical. We analyze environmental exposure, construction quality, and material behavior to determine why defects occurred.

Scope of Services

  • Visual condition surveys
  • Structural and material evaluation
  • Non-destructive testing (NDT)
  • Laboratory testing
  • Corrosion assessment
  • Documentation and reporting

Why Condition Assessment is Critical

Incorrect diagnosis often leads to ineffective repairs and recurring failures. A thorough assessment ensures that interventions are targeted and technically sound.

Key Outcomes

  • Clear understanding of structural condition
  • Identification of risks and deterioration mechanisms
  • Data-driven repair recommendations
  • Optimized maintenance planning

Applications

  • Residential and commercial buildings
  • Industrial facilities
  • Bridges and infrastructure
  • Marine and coastal structures
