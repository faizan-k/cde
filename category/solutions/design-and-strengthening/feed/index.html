Defects introduced during construction can significantly affect the long-term performance of a structure. Issues such as improper curing, honeycombing, and early-age cracking can compromise durability if not identified and addressed early.

Concrete evaluation during construction helps detect and correct such issues before they escalate.

What is Concrete Evaluation

Concrete evaluation is the process of assessing the quality and integrity of concrete during or shortly after construction to identify defects and ensure compliance with design requirements.

Our Approach

1. Visual Inspection

Concrete surfaces are inspected to identify visible defects such as cracks, voids, and surface irregularities.

2. Quality Assessment

Workmanship and construction practices are evaluated to identify potential causes of defects.

3. Testing and Verification

Testing methods are used to assess strength, uniformity, and integrity of concrete.

4. Defect Analysis

Identified defects are analyzed to determine their cause and potential impact.

5. Corrective Recommendations

Appropriate measures are recommended to address defects and prevent future issues.

Scope of Services

  • Identification of construction defects
  • Assessment of concrete quality
  • Testing and evaluation
  • Root cause analysis
  • Recommendations for corrective actions

Key Outcomes

  • Early detection of defects
  • Improved construction quality
  • Prevention of long-term deterioration
  • Enhanced structural performance

Applications

  • Newly constructed buildings
  • Infrastructure projects
  • Concrete structures under construction
  • Quality control programs

What is PT Structure Assessment

PT structure assessment evaluates the condition and performance of post-tensioning systems, including tendons, anchorages, and overall structural behavior.

Our Approach

1. Visual Inspection

Inspection of accessible PT components to identify signs of distress or damage.

2. Testing and Evaluation

Specialized techniques are used to assess tendon condition and detect corrosion or loss of prestress.

3. Structural Analysis

The behavior of the structure is evaluated to identify any deficiencies.

4. Risk Assessment

Potential risks associated with PT systems are identified and evaluated.

5. Repair Strategy Development

Appropriate repair or strengthening solutions are recommended.

Scope of Services

Structures are often required to perform beyond their original design conditions due to aging, increased loading, changes in usage, or environmental exposure. Over time, this can lead to reduced capacity, hidden weaknesses, or safety concerns.

A detailed structural assessment provides a clear understanding of how a structure is performing and whether it can safely meet current and future demands. When deficiencies are identified, strengthening solutions are required to restore or enhance performance.

What is Structural Assessment

Structural assessment is the process of evaluating the load-carrying capacity, stability, and behavior of a structure under existing and anticipated conditions.

It involves engineering analysis, understanding of material behavior, and evaluation of structural systems to determine whether the structure meets safety and performance requirements.

At CDE, structural assessment is carried out using a performance-based approach that reflects real-world conditions.

Our Approach

1. Data Collection

All relevant information is gathered, including drawings, material properties, and usage conditions. Site observations and historical data are also reviewed.

2. Structural Analysis

Advanced analytical tools are used to evaluate load paths, stress distribution, and deformation behavior under various conditions.

3. Capacity Evaluation

The structure is assessed against applicable standards to determine whether it can safely carry current and future loads.

4. Identification of Deficiencies

Weak zones, overstressed elements, and potential failure points are identified based on analysis and observations.

5. Strengthening Strategy Development

Appropriate strengthening solutions are developed to address deficiencies and improve performance.

Scope of Services

Structural Evaluation

  • Assessment of load-carrying capacity
  • Evaluation of structural behavior
  • Identification of critical deficiencies

Analysis and Modelling

  • Structural modelling
  • Simulation of loading conditions
  • Performance-based evaluation

Strengthening Design

  • Design of strengthening systems
  • Integration with existing structures
  • Preparation of drawings and specifications

Strengthening Solutions

  • Carbon fiber reinforcement systems
  • Steel plate bonding
  • Section enlargement
  • Load redistribution techniques

Key Outcomes

  • Enhanced structural safety
  • Improved load capacity
  • Compliance with standards
  • Extended service life

Applications

  • Structures with increased load requirements
  • Aging buildings and infrastructure
  • Change of use projects
  • Performance upgrades

Engineering Value

Structural assessment and strengthening provide a cost-effective way to enhance performance and extend the life of existing assets while ensuring safety and reliability.
