CDE Engineering Services was engaged by SPD Interior & Landscaping Contracting to carry out a structural assessment and develop strengthening design solutions for an existing villa located in Dubai Hills.

The project involved evaluating the impact of proposed architectural modifications, including the addition of a new steel roof structure and changes at basement and roof levels, on the existing structural system.

Client

SPD Interior & Landscaping Contracting

Location

Dubai, UAE

Client Challenge

The client planned multiple architectural modifications, including the addition of new structural elements such as a steel roof, jacuzzi, and technical rooms.

These changes introduced additional loads and required careful assessment to ensure that the existing structural system, including slabs and foundations, could safely accommodate the modifications without compromising stability.

Scope of Work

  • Structural assessment of existing building
  • Evaluation of load capacity for proposed modifications
  • Analysis of impact of additional steel roof structure
  • Design of strengthening measures
  • Preparation of structural design solutions for modifications

Our Approach

CDE carried out a detailed structural evaluation combining review of existing drawings and analytical modelling.

The assessment focused on understanding the interaction between existing structural elements and proposed modifications. Structural analysis was performed to evaluate load transfer mechanisms and identify areas requiring strengthening.

Design solutions were developed to ensure compatibility between new and existing structural systems while maintaining overall structural integrity.

Key Findings

The introduction of a new steel roof and additional architectural elements significantly increased loading on the existing structure.

Critical areas identified included slab sections subjected to punching shear, beams affected by demolition and modification works, and zones requiring additional support for new structural elements.

The existing structure required localized strengthening to safely accommodate the proposed changes.

Solutions & Recommendations

CDE developed a comprehensive strengthening design to integrate new architectural features while maintaining structural integrity.

Strengthening measures included reinforcement of slabs to resist punching shear, strengthening of beams affected by modifications, and introduction of new beams and supports where required.

Additional structural provisions were made to support new elements such as steel columns, staircase openings, and cantilever projections.

The design ensured safe load transfer and compatibility between new and existing structural components.

Outcome

The project enabled seamless integration of architectural modifications without compromising structural safety.

Through detailed analysis and tailored strengthening design, CDE ensured that the villa could accommodate additional loads and functional enhancements while maintaining structural performance and reliability.

Key Services Delivered

  • Load Capacity Evaluation
  • Structural Assessment
  • Structural Analysis
  • Strengthening Design
  • Modification Design Integration

CDE Engineering Services was engaged by Land Sterling to perform a comprehensive structural review and condition assessment of an existing warehouse located in Jebel Ali Free Zone (JAFZA), Dubai.

The assessment was carried out prior to the client’s decision to acquire the asset, with the objective of evaluating structural integrity, compliance with design standards, and long-term performance.

Client

Land Sterling

Location

Dubai, UAE

Client Challenge

The client required an independent technical evaluation of the warehouse to support an informed investment decision.

Key concerns included the structural condition of steel elements, potential corrosion, load-carrying capacity, and compliance with relevant codal requirements. The client needed clarity on any existing deficiencies and associated risks before proceeding with acquisition.

Scope of Work

  • On-site structural inspection of steel elements
  • Review of structural system and existing condition
  • Structural analysis through STAAD model review
  • Code compliance evaluation
  • Identification of structural risks and deficiencies
  • Development of strengthening and maintenance recommendations

Our Approach

CDE carried out a multi-stage evaluation combining field inspection and analytical review.

On-site inspection was conducted to assess the condition of steel structural elements, focusing on identifying signs of distress, corrosion, and degradation.

In parallel, the structural design was evaluated through a detailed review of the STAAD model to verify load-carrying capacity and compliance with applicable codes.

The assessment aimed to correlate physical observations with analytical findings to ensure a comprehensive understanding of structural performance.

Key Findings

The evaluation identified localized areas of distress in steel elements, including signs of minor corrosion. While not critical at present, these issues could lead to long-term deterioration if left unaddressed.

Structural analysis highlighted specific elements and connections requiring reinforcement to improve load distribution and ensure adequate safety margins.

The review also emphasized the importance of proactive maintenance to prevent further degradation and maintain structural performance.

Solutions & Recommendations

CDE recommended targeted strengthening and maintenance measures to enhance structural integrity and durability.

Strengthening of identified weak elements and connections was proposed to improve load-carrying capacity.

Corrosion protection measures were recommended to mitigate environmental effects and extend service life.

A periodic monitoring and maintenance strategy was advised to ensure long-term performance and prevent future deterioration.

Additionally, updating as-built documentation was recommended to ensure compliance with current standards and facilitate future assessments.

Outcome

The assessment provided the client with a clear and reliable understanding of the structural condition of the warehouse.

The findings and recommendations enabled the client to make an informed investment decision, supported by a clear roadmap for strengthening, maintenance, and long-term asset management.

Key Services Delivered

  • Maintenance Strategy Development
  • Structural Condition Assessment
  • Structural Analysis & Model Review (STAAD)
  • Code Compliance Evaluation
  • Risk Identification
  • Strengthening Recommendations
